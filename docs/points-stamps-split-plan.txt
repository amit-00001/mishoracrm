0. YEH KYON — CONTEXT
============================================================
Owner ne stampzo.in ko reference diya customer loyalty + revisit ke liye
(6 pages dekhe: /about, /qr-kit, /features, /cafes, aur 2 blog posts). Note:
sirf marketing pages the — actual app ke screens nahi dekhe, isliye neeche
sirf FLOW/philosophy li gayi hai, UI copy nahi.

Stampzo ka core:
  - DO ALAG QR: shop ka printed QR sirf JOIN karata hai (card phone mein aata
    hai, koi app nahi). Stamp customer ke PHONE ke QR se lagta hai, jise staff
    apne phone se scan karta hai. "Shop print = join. Phone card = stamps."
    Staff kabhi shop poster scan karke stamp nahi lagata.
  - Staff scan -> screen pe customer ka NAAM -> ek tap = stamp (~2 sec). Naam
    isliye dikhta hai ki staff face se match kare (fraud check). Card login se
    bandha hai, photocopy/marker se fake nahi hota.
  - Ek simple offer: "Buy 4, get 5th free". Points/tiers nahi. Focus single-shop,
    single-offer.
  - Free item pehle diya jaata hai, phir card reset. Stamp payment ke BAAD.
  - Notifications: reward ready + "reminder if they disappear" (revisit nudge).
  - QR Kit: window poster, counter stand, board, table tent, flyer — shop naam,
    brand colour, offer, visit count daalke print-ready PDF.
  - Launch same-day: reward likho, A4 print, staff ko 5-step photo guide.
    Pehla program free, phir flat monthly INR fee.
  - Blog ka insight: "A 20% code trains people to wait. A digital stamp card
    trains them to come back." (coupon = discount-hunter, stamp = habit.)

    (Storage jahan Tenant::loyaltySettings() padhta hai wahi rahega.)

      only loyalty ON          -> points

10. REAL-WORLD REVISIT — EVIDENCE + REVISED RECOMMENDATION (2026-09-26)
============================================================
STATUS: sifarish hai — owner ne confirm NAHI kiya.

EVIDENCE (kya mila, kitna bharosa):
  - Endowed progress (Nunes & Dreze 2006, Journal of Consumer Research): car
    wash, 300 cards, dono mein same 8 purchases chahiye. Card A = 10 spaces, 2
    pehle se stamped; Card B = 8 khali. 9 mahine mein 34% ne reward liya (A)
    vs 19% (B).
  - Goal-gradient (Kivetz, Urminsky, Zheng 2006, Journal of Marketing
    Research): cafe "buy 10 get 1 free" — reward ke jitna kareeb, utni jaldi-
    jaldi visit. Pehla reward milne ke baad engagement GIRTI hai, doosre
    reward ke paas phir badhti hai. 12-stamp card (2 bonus stamps) ne 10
    purchases regular 10-stamp card se tezi se poori karwayi.
  - Stamp vs points: vendor blogs (BonusQR, StampMe, LoyaltyPass...) — stamps
    fixed-ticket + frequent-visit shops ke liye, points variable-spend ke
    liye. Yeh MARKETING content hai, data nahi — sirf direction.
  CAVEATS: dono studies US (car wash / cafe), chhota sample. India kirana/
  salon ka data nahi mila. Stampzo ke pages mein bhi koi revisit statistic
  nahi. Isliye har cheez SETTING rakho (on/off) aur measure karo (loyalty
  overview mein active cards / rewards unlocked pehle se hai).
  SOURCES:
    https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/abs/10.1509/jmkr.43.1.39
    https://siliconcanals.com/t-car-wash-loyalty-cards-endowed-progress/
    https://www.stampme.com/blog/stamps-vs-points-loyalty-programs

REAL-WORLD SE NIKLE FAISLE:
  A. Program shop-type se: fixed ticket + frequent (chai, cafe, salon haircut,
     kirana daily) -> STAMPS. Variable spend (garments, electronics, pharmacy,
     restaurant bills) -> POINTS. Isliye ek shop = ek program (R1) sahi hai;
     dono chalane se staff (2-sec counter) aur customer dono confuse.
  B. CARD CHHOTA: 5-6 stamps (Stampzo "buy 4 get 5th free" bhi). Pehla reward
     2-3 hafte mein milna chahiye, warna interest mar jaata hai.
  C. WELCOME STAMP (endowed progress): join pe 1 stamp ek baar (per phone per
     shop) — card 6 ka, 1 pehle se bhara, 5 visit baaki. Yeh Section 4.1/4.8 ke
     purane "join kabhi stamp nahi deta" ko REVISE karta hai. Fraud-safe:
     sirf phone-verified WhatsApp join se, ek baar per contact, repeat JOIN
     pe kuch nahi; poster scan se repeat-stamping phir bhi impossible.
     Setting `welcome_stamps` (stamps shop default 1, 0 = off). Tab test
     StampCardTest::test_joining_through_the_welcome_qr_never_gives_a_stamp
     badlega -> "join sirf configured welcome stamps ek baar deta hai, repeat
     JOIN kuch nahi".
  D. REWARD KE BAAD RESTART: pehla reward milne ke baad engagement girti hai.
     Naya card 1 stamp bhara shuru ho (optional `restart_stamps`), aur redeem
     hote hi customer ko WhatsApp: "new card started, 1/6".
  E. NEAR-COMPLETE NUDGE ka priority BADHAO (goal-gradient ka seedha fayda).
     Section 4.5 Step 5 se Step 4 mein. Trigger: card >= 80% bhara / 1 stamp
     bacha AND N din se visit nahi.
  F. CHANNEL: customer din mein kai baar WhatsApp kholta hai — card/nudge
